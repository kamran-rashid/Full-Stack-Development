    Let it compound

    A = P × (1 + r/n) ^ (n × t)

    Reading the formula

    A
    What you end up with.
    P
    The amount you start with (the principal).
    r
    The yearly return as a fraction: 10% is 0.10.
    n
    How many times a year the interest is added: 1 for yearly, 12 for monthly.
    t
    The number of years you leave it alone.
